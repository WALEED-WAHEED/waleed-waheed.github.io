Delete your Desi Calories account

Desi Calories by Waleed Waheed

An account is optional in Desi Calories. If you created one to back up your diary, you can delete it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Desi Calories and go to Profile → Account and backup.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email waleedwaheed1081@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in records).
  • Your cloud backup (diary, weights, foods, recipes, settings and profile).
  • Your daily photo-check counts.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records held by Google Play.

What is not deleted automatically

  • The diary stored on your phone. Remove it with Profile → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.
  • A Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
