Desi Calories

The calorie and protein tracker built for desi food, and every other food too.

  • Roti, daal, sabzi, karahi, biryani, chai and over 180 more desi dishes, in katoris, plates and pieces.
  • Tell it how much oil or ghee went in; it's the biggest difference between home-cooked meals.
  • Over 16,000 everyday foods from USDA FoodData Central and the UK government's CoFID lab data, plus a free barcode scanner.
  • Type a whole meal in one line, like "2 roti, aadha katori daal, chai".
  • Every value shows where it came from. No ads. Your diary stays on your phone.

Desi Calories gives general nutrition information and is not medical advice.
