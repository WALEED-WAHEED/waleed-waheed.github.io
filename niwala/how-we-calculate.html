How we calculate

Every desi dish in Niwala is modelled from a typical home recipe, ingredient by ingredient, using public lab data. The oil or ghee is counted separately, because it changes a curry's calories more than anything else.

1. A real recipe, weighed

For each of the 191 dishes we write a typical home recipe for one pot: every ingredient in grams, raw. Chicken karahi, for example, is chicken, tomato, onion, ginger, garlic, green chilli, spices and oil.

2. Lab values for every ingredient

Each ingredient's calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar and sodium come from USDA FoodData Central, the US government's public food composition database. Where USDA has no exact match we use the closest stand-in and record it (for example, carp for rohu). We check each recipe's total against the standard 4-4-9 rule (protein, carbs and fat) so a typo cannot slip through.

3. Divided into real portions

The cooked pot is divided into the portions people actually eat: a roti, a piece, a plate, or a katori, which we take as about 150 g of cooked food. You can change the portion and amount every time you log.

4. Oil and ghee are your choice

Home cooking varies most in the oil. Each curry shows how much oil or ghee our recipe assumes per portion, and lets you pick less, more, or a takeaway amount (about twice the home recipe, because restaurants usually cook richer). The calories update as you choose.

5. Everyday and packaged foods

Beyond desi dishes, Niwala includes over 16,000 foods from USDA FoodData Central and the UK's McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods (CoFID). Packaged foods come from the barcode on the pack, via Open Food Facts; we reject entries with missing or impossible values.

6. What the photo AI does, and doesn't

When you log a meal from a photo, AI only names the dishes and guesses portions. The calories always come from our data above, never from the AI, and you check every item before it is saved.

The honest limits

  • These are careful estimates for home-style cooking, not a lab test of your plate. Your recipe, portion and oil will differ.
  • Our desi recipes have not yet been reviewed by a registered dietitian. That review is planned; until then every dish is labelled as an estimate in the app.
  • Niwala gives general nutrition information and is not medical advice.
