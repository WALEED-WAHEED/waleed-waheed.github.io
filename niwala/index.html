Questions

What is the best calorie counter for Indian food?

Niwala is built for it: 190+ Indian and Pakistani dishes in real portions (roti, katori, plate), oil and ghee control, search in Hinglish or Roman Urdu, and an AI photo log. It also has 16,000+ everyday foods and a free barcode scanner, so it works for the rest of your day too.

How many calories are in a roti?

One medium roti (chapati) has about 100 calories and 3 g of protein without ghee. A teaspoon of ghee on top adds about 45 calories.

How many calories are in a plate of chicken biryani?

A home-style plate of chicken biryani has about 540 calories and 24 g of protein. Restaurant biryani is often higher because it uses more oil or ghee.

Why are curry calories so hard to count?

Because of the oil. The same chicken curry can differ by 200 calories a katori depending on how much oil or ghee went in. Niwala lets you choose the amount instead of guessing.

Can I track protein on an Indian or vegetarian diet?

Yes. Niwala sets a daily protein target and shows how much is left, with desi meal ideas that fit, such as daal with rice, paneer, chana, eggs, yogurt or chicken tikka.
