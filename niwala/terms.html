Health and accuracy

The app gives general nutrition information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have or had an eating disorder, or manage a condition such as diabetes, kidney disease or heart disease.

Nutrition values are estimates:

  • USDA values are averages; your brand or recipe can differ.
  • Desi dish values are calculated from typical home recipes. Home cooking varies a lot, especially oil and ghee, so each dish shows its assumption and lets you change it.
  • Product labels from Open Food Facts are entered by volunteers and can be wrong. Check them against your pack.
  • Photo logging uses AI to suggest foods and portions. It can be wrong; check each item before saving.
  • Daily targets come from a standard formula (Mifflin-St Jeor) and are a starting point, not a prescription.
