Bhuna gosht calories and protein

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of bhuna gosht, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Mutton (goat), raw: 110 g
  • Onion, raw: 40 g
  • Oil: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is bhuna gosht good for protein?

Bhuna gosht gives 8.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 93% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
