Questions

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 136 kcal to 401 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in bhuna gosht?

About 23 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 34% of its calories.
