Chicken tikka masala calories and protein

Also called: tikka masala, چکن تکہ مسالہ, चिकन टिक्का मसाला

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chicken tikka masala and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken tikka masala, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken breast, raw: 100 g
  • Tomato, raw: 50 g
  • Onion, raw: 30 g
  • Cream heavy: 15 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken tikka masala good for protein?

Chicken tikka masala gives 8.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 90% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chicken tikka masala high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 178 kcal to 390 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in chicken tikka masala?

About 24 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 33% of its calories.
