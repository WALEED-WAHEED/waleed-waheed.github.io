Desi omelette calories and protein

Also called: omlet, آملیٹ, ऑमलेट, anda omelette, omelet, anday ka omelette, omelette

1 piece (2 eggs) of desi omelette has about 196 calories and 13 g protein, with 2.7 g carbs and 15 g fat, cooked with 5 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's desi omelette and another's. For 1 piece (2 eggs):

OilCalories
0 g152
3 g178
5 g (our standard)196
10 g240

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece (2 eggs) of desi omelette, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Egg whole, raw: 100 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Oil: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is desi omelette good for protein?

Desi omelette gives 6.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 82% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is desi omelette high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece (2 eggs) ranges from about 152 kcal to 240 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in desi omelette?

About 13 g of protein in 1 piece (2 eggs), which is 26% of its calories.
