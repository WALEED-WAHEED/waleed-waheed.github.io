Chole bhature plate calories and protein

Also called: chole bhature, chana bhatura, چھولے بھٹورے, छोले भटूरे

1 plate (2 bhature) of chole bhature plate has about 845 calories and 21 g protein, with 109 g carbs and 36 g fat, cooked with 32 g of oil.

Questions

Is chole bhature plate high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate (2 bhature) ranges from about 562 kcal to 1,127 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 64 g.

How much protein is in chole bhature plate?

About 21 g of protein in 1 plate (2 bhature), which is 10% of its calories.
