For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chana chaat, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

Is chana chaat good for protein?

Chana chaat gives 5.0 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 69% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
