Beef biryani calories and protein

Also called: beef biriyani, بیف بریانی

1 plate of beef biryani has about 549 calories and 26 g protein, with 65 g carbs and 20 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's beef biryani and another's. For 1 plate:

OilCalories
0 g417
8 g488

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 plate of beef biryani, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Beef chuck, raw: 90 g
  • Basmati rice, raw: 75 g
  • Onion, raw: 30 g
  • Tomato, raw: 20 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 20 g
  • Oil: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is beef biryani good for protein?

Beef biryani gives 4.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 67% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is beef biryani high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 417 kcal to 682 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in beef biryani?

About 26 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 19% of its calories.
