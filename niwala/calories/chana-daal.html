Chana daal calories and protein

Also called: chana dal, chane ki daal, چنے کی دال, चना दाल

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chana daal has about 201 calories and 7.4 g protein, with 24 g carbs and 9.2 g fat, cooked with 7 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chana daal and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g139
3 g166
7 g (our standard)201
12 g245
14 g263

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chana daal, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chickpeas (chana), dry: 35 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Onion, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 7 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chana daal good for protein?

Chana daal gives 3.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 51% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chana daal high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 139 kcal to 263 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 14 g.

How much protein is in chana daal?

About 7.4 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 15% of its calories.
