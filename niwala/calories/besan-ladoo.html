Besan ladoo calories and protein

Also called: laddu, ladoo, بیسن لڈو, बेसन लड्डू

1 piece of besan ladoo has about 150 calories and 2.9 g protein, with 18 g carbs and 7.9 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of besan ladoo, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Besan (gram flour): 13 g
  • Sugar: 10 g
  • Ghee: 7 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is besan ladoo good for protein?

Besan ladoo gives 1.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 18% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
