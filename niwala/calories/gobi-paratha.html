Gobi paratha calories and protein

Also called: gobhi paratha, cauliflower paratha, گوبھی پراٹھا, गोभी पराठा

1 piece of gobi paratha has about 244 calories and 5.4 g protein, with 39 g carbs and 9.1 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's gobi paratha and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g174
4 g209
8 g (our standard)244
12 g279
16 g314

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is gobi paratha high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 174 kcal to 314 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in gobi paratha?

About 5.4 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 9% of its calories.
