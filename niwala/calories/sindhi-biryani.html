Sindhi biryani calories and protein

Also called: sindhi biriyani, سندھی بریانی, सिंधी बिरयानी

1 plate of sindhi biryani has about 602 calories and 25 g protein, with 72 g carbs and 23 g fat, cooked with 18 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's sindhi biryani and another's. For 1 plate:

OilCalories
0 g443
10 g531
18 g (our standard)602
25 g664
36 g761

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 plate of sindhi biryani, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken thigh, raw: 90 g
  • Basmati rice, raw: 75 g
  • Potato, raw: 40 g
  • Onion, raw: 30 g
  • Tomato, raw: 30 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 20 g
  • Oil: 18 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is sindhi biryani good for protein?

Sindhi biryani gives 4.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 58% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps

Instead of 1 plate of sindhi biryani (602 kcal):

  • Chicken pulao: 493 kcal (−109), 22 g protein
  • Yakhni pulao: 464 kcal (−138), 20 g protein
  • Chicken fried rice: 483 kcal (−119), 21 g protein

Questions

Is sindhi biryani high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 443 kcal to 761 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 36 g.

How much protein is in sindhi biryani?

About 25 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 17% of its calories.
