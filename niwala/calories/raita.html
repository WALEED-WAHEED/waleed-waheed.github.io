The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of raita, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Yogurt (dahi): 120 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is raita good for protein?

Raita gives 5.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 77% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
