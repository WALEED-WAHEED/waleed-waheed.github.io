Chicken manchurian calories and protein

Also called: manchurian, chicken manchoorian, چکن منچورین, चिकन मंचूरियन

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken manchurian has about 321 calories and 25 g protein, with 24 g carbs and 14 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

Questions

Is chicken manchurian high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 214 kcal to 427 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in chicken manchurian?

About 25 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 31% of its calories.
