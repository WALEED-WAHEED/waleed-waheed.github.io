Is chicken manchurian good for protein?

Chicken manchurian gives 7.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 87% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

How much protein is in chicken manchurian?

About 25 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 31% of its calories.
