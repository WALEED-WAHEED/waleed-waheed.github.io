Tahiri calories and protein

Also called: tehri, aloo tahiri, تہری, तहरी

1 plate of tahiri has about 434 calories and 7.5 g protein, with 72 g carbs and 13 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's tahiri and another's. For 1 plate:

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is tahiri high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 328 kcal to 541 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in tahiri?

About 7.5 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 7% of its calories.
