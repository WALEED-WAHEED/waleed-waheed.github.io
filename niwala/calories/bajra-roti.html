Bajra roti calories and protein

Also called: bajre ki roti, millet roti, bajra rotla, باجرے کی روٹی, बाजरे की रोटी

1 piece of bajra roti has about 153 calories and 4.3 g protein, with 30 g carbs and 1.7 g fat.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee on top is the biggest difference between one home's bajra roti and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g (our standard)153
3 g179
5 g197

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is bajra roti high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 153 kcal to 197 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 5 g.

How much protein is in bajra roti?

About 4.3 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 11% of its calories.
