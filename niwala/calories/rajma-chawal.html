Rajma chawal plate calories and protein

Also called: rajma rice, راجما چاول, राजमा चावल

1 plate of rajma chawal plate has about 420 calories and 14 g protein, with 71 g carbs and 8.9 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's rajma chawal plate and another's. For 1 plate:

OilCalories
0 g350
4 g385
8 g (our standard)420
12 g456
16 g491

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 plate of rajma chawal plate, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 50 g
  • Rajma (kidney beans), dry: 45 g
  • Tomato, raw: 30 g
  • Onion, raw: 25 g
  • Oil: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is rajma chawal plate good for protein?

Rajma chawal plate gives 3.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 46% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is rajma chawal plate high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 350 kcal to 491 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in rajma chawal plate?

About 14 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 14% of its calories.
