Saag calories and protein

Also called: palak, sarson ka saag, ساگ, साग, sag, sarson, sarson saag

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of saag has about 111 calories and 4.5 g protein, with 6.8 g carbs and 8.6 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or butter is the biggest difference between one home's saag and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

GheeCalories
0 g40
4 g76
8 g (our standard)111
15 g172
16 g181

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of saag, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Spinach, raw: 150 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Ghee: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is saag good for protein?

Saag gives 4.1 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 58% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is saag high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 40 kcal to 181 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in saag?

About 4.5 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 16% of its calories.
