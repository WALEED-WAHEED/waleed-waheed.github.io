Rabri calories and protein

Also called: rabdi, ربڑی, रबड़ी

Half katori of rabri has about 178 calories and 6.5 g protein, with 24 g carbs and 6.4 g fat, with 15 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's rabri and another's. For half katori:

SugarCalories
0 g120
8 g151
15 g (our standard)178
22 g205
30 g236

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is rabri high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. Half katori ranges from about 120 kcal to 236 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in rabri?

About 6.5 g of protein in half katori, which is 15% of its calories.
