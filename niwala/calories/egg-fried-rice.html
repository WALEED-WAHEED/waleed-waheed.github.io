Egg fried rice calories and protein

Also called: fried rice, انڈا فرائیڈ رائس, एग फ्राइड राइस

1 plate of egg fried rice has about 452 calories and 13 g protein, with 64 g carbs and 15 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's egg fried rice and another's. For 1 plate:

OilCalories
0 g364
5 g408
10 g (our standard)452
15 g496
20 g540

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 plate of egg fried rice, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 75 g
  • Egg whole, raw: 50 g
  • Peas green, raw: 15 g
  • Carrot, raw: 15 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is egg fried rice good for protein?

Egg fried rice gives 2.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 35% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is egg fried rice high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 364 kcal to 540 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in egg fried rice?

About 13 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 11% of its calories.
