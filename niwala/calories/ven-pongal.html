Ven pongal calories and protein

Also called: pongal, khara pongal, پونگل, पोंगल

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of ven pongal has about 248 calories and 6.3 g protein, with 34 g carbs and 9.7 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's ven pongal and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is ven pongal high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 178 kcal to 318 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in ven pongal?

About 6.3 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 10% of its calories.
