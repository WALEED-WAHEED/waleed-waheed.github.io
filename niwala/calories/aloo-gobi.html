Aloo gobi calories and protein

Also called: potato cauliflower, آلو گوبھی, आलू गोभी, gobi aloo, aloo gobhi, gobhi

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of aloo gobi has about 169 calories and 3.1 g protein, with 18 g carbs and 10 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's aloo gobi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g81
5 g125
10 g (our standard)169
15 g214
20 g258

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of aloo gobi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 70 g
  • Cauliflower, raw: 70 g
  • Tomato, raw: 20 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is aloo gobi good for protein?

Aloo gobi gives 1.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 15% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is aloo gobi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 81 kcal to 258 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in aloo gobi?

About 3.1 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 7% of its calories.
