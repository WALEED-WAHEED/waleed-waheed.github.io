Roti calories and protein

Also called: chapati, phulka, روٹی, रोटी, chapatti, tawa roti, rotti, fulka

1 piece of roti has about 100 calories and 2.9 g protein, with 22 g carbs and 0.6 g fat.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece1002.9 g22 g0.6 g3.9 g

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee on top is the biggest difference between one home's roti and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g (our standard)100
3 g126
5 g143

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of roti, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Atta (whole wheat flour): 30 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is roti good for protein?

Roti gives 2.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 39% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps

Instead of 1 piece of roti (100 kcal):

  • Idli: 61 kcal (−38), 1.9 g protein

Questions

Is roti high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 100 kcal to 143 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 5 g.

How much protein is in roti?

About 2.9 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 12% of its calories.
