Aloo keema calories and protein

Also called: aloo qeema, keema aloo, آلو قیمہ, आलू कीमा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of aloo keema has about 302 calories and 14 g protein, with 12 g carbs and 22 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

Questions

Is aloo keema high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 232 kcal to 373 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in aloo keema?

About 14 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 18% of its calories.
