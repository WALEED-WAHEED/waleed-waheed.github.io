Kulcha calories and protein

Also called: amritsari kulcha, کلچہ, कुलचा

1 piece of kulcha has about 260 calories and 6.5 g protein, with 46 g carbs and 5 g fat, cooked with 5 g of butter.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece2606.5 g46 g5 g1.6 g

How much does the butter change it?

A lot. Butter is the biggest difference between one home's kulcha and another's. For 1 piece:

ButterCalories
0 g224
5 g (our standard)260
10 g296

Each gram of butter adds about 7.2 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of kulcha, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Maida (white flour): 60 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 10 g
  • Butter: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kulcha good for protein?

Kulcha gives 2.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 28% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of kulcha (260 kcal):

  • Missi roti: 185 kcal (−75), 5.8 g protein
  • Roti: 100 kcal (−161), 2.9 g protein
  • Tandoori roti: 149 kcal (−111), 4.3 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Tandoori chicken: 236 kcal (−24), 29 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−137), 13 g protein

Questions

Is kulcha high in calories?

It depends mostly on the butter. 1 piece ranges from about 224 kcal to 296 kcal as the butter goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in kulcha?

About 6.5 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 10% of its calories.
