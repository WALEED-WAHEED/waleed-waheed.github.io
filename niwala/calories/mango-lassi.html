Mango lassi calories and protein

Also called: aam lassi, مینگو لسی, मैंगो लस्सी

1 glass of mango lassi has about 197 calories and 5.9 g protein, with 34 g carbs and 5.2 g fat, with 15 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's mango lassi and another's. For 1 glass:

SugarCalories
0 g140
8 g170
15 g (our standard)197
30 g255

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 glass of mango lassi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Yogurt (dahi): 150 g
  • Mango, raw: 80 g
  • Sugar: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is mango lassi good for protein?

Mango lassi gives 3.0 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 40% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is mango lassi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 glass ranges from about 140 kcal to 255 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in mango lassi?

About 5.9 g of protein in 1 glass, which is 12% of its calories.
