Missi roti calories and protein

Also called: besan roti, مسی روٹی, मिस्सी रोटी

1 piece of missi roti has about 185 calories and 5.8 g protein, with 27 g carbs and 6.5 g fat, cooked with 5 g of ghee.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece1855.8 g27 g6.5 g4.9 g

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's missi roti and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g141
5 g (our standard)185
10 g229

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of missi roti, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Atta (whole wheat flour): 25 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 15 g
  • Ghee: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is missi roti good for protein?

Missi roti gives 3.1 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 43% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of missi roti (185 kcal):

  • Roti: 100 kcal (−85), 2.9 g protein
  • Tandoori roti: 149 kcal (−35), 4.3 g protein
  • Rumali roti: 127 kcal (−57), 3.6 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−62), 13 g protein
  • Shami kebab: 123 kcal (−62), 8.2 g protein

Questions

Is missi roti high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 141 kcal to 229 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in missi roti?

About 5.8 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 13% of its calories.
