Falooda calories and protein

Also called: faluda, kulfi falooda, فالودہ, फालूदा

1 glass of falooda has about 356 calories and 10 g protein, with 52 g carbs and 12 g fat, with 20 g of sugar.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 glass35610 g52 g12 g0.8 g

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Rose syrup / sugar is the biggest difference between one home's falooda and another's. For 1 glass:

SugarCalories
0 g279
10 g318
20 g (our standard)356
30 g395
40 g433

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 glass of falooda, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 200 g
  • Ice cream vanilla: 50 g
  • Vermicelli, dry: 15 g
  • Sugar: 20 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is falooda good for protein?

Falooda gives 2.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 38% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps

Instead of 1 glass of falooda (356 kcal):

  • Namkeen lassi: 122 kcal (−234), 6.9 g protein
  • Badam doodh: 242 kcal (−114), 9.9 g protein
  • Sweet lassi: 229 kcal (−127), 8.6 g protein

Questions

Is falooda high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 glass ranges from about 279 kcal to 433 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 40 g.

How much protein is in falooda?

About 10 g of protein in 1 glass, which is 11% of its calories.
