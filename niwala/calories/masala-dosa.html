Masala dosa calories and protein

Also called: مسالہ ڈوسا, मसाला डोसा

1 piece of masala dosa has about 271 calories and 6.1 g protein, with 43 g carbs and 8.4 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's masala dosa and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g200
4 g236
8 g (our standard)271
12 g307
16 g342

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of masala dosa, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 80 g
  • Basmati rice, raw: 28 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Urad (mash) daal, dry: 9 g
  • Oil: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is masala dosa good for protein?

Masala dosa gives 2.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 23% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of masala dosa (271 kcal):

  • Medu vada: 139 kcal (−132), 5 g protein
  • Idli: 61 kcal (−210), 1.9 g protein
  • Plain dosa: 168 kcal (−103), 4.3 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Tandoori chicken: 236 kcal (−35), 29 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−148), 13 g protein

Questions

Is masala dosa high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 200 kcal to 342 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in masala dosa?

About 6.1 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 9% of its calories.
