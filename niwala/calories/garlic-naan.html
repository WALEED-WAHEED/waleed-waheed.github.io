Garlic naan calories and protein

Also called: lehsan naan, garlic nan, گارلک نان, गार्लिक नान

1 piece of garlic naan has about 405 calories and 10 g protein, with 71 g carbs and 8.1 g fat, cooked with 8 g of butter.

How much does the butter change it?

A lot. Butter on top is the biggest difference between one home's garlic naan and another's. For 1 piece:

ButterCalories
0 g347
5 g383
8 g (our standard)405
12 g433
16 g462

Each gram of butter adds about 7.2 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is garlic naan high in calories?

It depends mostly on the butter. 1 piece ranges from about 347 kcal to 462 kcal as the butter goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in garlic naan?

About 10 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 10% of its calories.
