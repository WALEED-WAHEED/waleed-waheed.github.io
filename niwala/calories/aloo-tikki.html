Aloo tikki calories and protein

Also called: aloo cutlet, potato cutlet, آلو ٹکی, आलू टिक्की

1 piece of aloo tikki has about 125 calories and 1.7 g protein, with 16 g carbs and 6.1 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece1251.7 g16 g6.1 g1.6 g

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's aloo tikki and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g72
3 g99
6 g (our standard)125
10 g161
12 g178

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of aloo tikki, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 70 g
  • Rice flour: 5 g
  • Oil: 6 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is aloo tikki good for protein?

Aloo tikki gives 1.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 5% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of aloo tikki (125 kcal):

  • Idli: 61 kcal (−64), 1.9 g protein
  • Roti: 100 kcal (−26), 2.9 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−2), 13 g protein

Questions

Is aloo tikki high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 72 kcal to 178 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in aloo tikki?

About 1.7 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 5% of its calories.
