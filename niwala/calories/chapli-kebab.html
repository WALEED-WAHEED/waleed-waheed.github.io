Chapli kebab calories and protein

Also called: chappal kabab, peshawari kebab, چپلی کباب, चपली कबाब

1 piece of chapli kebab has about 340 calories and 17 g protein, with 2.1 g carbs and 29 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's chapli kebab and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g252
5 g296
10 g (our standard)340
15 g384
20 g428

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of chapli kebab, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Beef ground, raw: 90 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Egg whole, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chapli kebab good for protein?

Chapli kebab gives 5.0 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 69% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chapli kebab high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 252 kcal to 428 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in chapli kebab?

About 17 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 20% of its calories.
