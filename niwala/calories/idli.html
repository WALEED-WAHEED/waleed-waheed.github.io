Idli calories and protein

Also called: idly, اڈلی, इडली

1 piece of idli has about 61 calories and 1.9 g protein, with 13 g carbs and 0.2 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of idli, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 13 g
  • Urad (mash) daal, dry: 4 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is idli good for protein?

Idli gives 3.1 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 43% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
