The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of cham cham, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Paneer: 22 g
  • Sugar: 20 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is cham cham good for protein?

Cham cham gives 3.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 50% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
