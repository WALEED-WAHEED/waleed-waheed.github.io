Kachori calories and protein

Also called: khasta kachori, moong kachori, کچوری, कचौरी

1 piece of kachori has about 250 calories and 5.5 g protein, with 29 g carbs and 12 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece2505.5 g29 g12 g2.4 g

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's kachori and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g144
8 g215
12 g (our standard)250
16 g285
24 g356

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of kachori, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Maida (white flour): 30 g
  • Moong daal, dry: 10 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kachori good for protein?

Kachori gives 2.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 21% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is kachori high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 144 kcal to 356 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in kachori?

About 5.5 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 9% of its calories.
