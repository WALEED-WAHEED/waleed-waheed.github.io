Pakora calories and protein

Also called: pakoray, bhajia, پکوڑے, पकौड़ा, pakoda, pakore, pakode, bhajiya

1 serving (5 pieces) of pakora has about 319 calories and 9.8 g protein, with 30 g carbs and 18 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed (fried) is the biggest difference between one home's pakora and another's. For 1 serving (5 pieces):

OilCalories
0 g186
10 g275
15 g (our standard)319
20 g363
30 g451

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving (5 pieces) of pakora, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Besan (gram flour): 40 g
  • Onion, raw: 40 g
  • Potato, raw: 20 g
  • Oil: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is pakora good for protein?

Pakora gives 3.1 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 42% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is pakora high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving (5 pieces) ranges from about 186 kcal to 451 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in pakora?

About 9.8 g of protein in 1 serving (5 pieces), which is 12% of its calories.
