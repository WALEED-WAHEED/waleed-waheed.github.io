Khichdi calories and protein

Also called: khichri, کھچڑی, खिचड़ी, khichari, khichrri

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of khichdi has about 204 calories and 6.6 g protein, with 32 g carbs and 5.4 g fat, cooked with 5 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's khichdi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

GheeCalories
0 g161
5 g (our standard)204
10 g248

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of khichdi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 25 g
  • Moong daal, dry: 20 g
  • Ghee: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is khichdi good for protein?

Khichdi gives 3.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 44% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is khichdi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 161 kcal to 248 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in khichdi?

About 6.6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 13% of its calories.
