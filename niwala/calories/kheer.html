Kheer calories and protein

Also called: rice pudding, کھیر, खीर, khir, chawal ki kheer

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kheer has about 203 calories and 6 g protein, with 34 g carbs and 4.9 g fat, with 15 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's kheer and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

SugarCalories
0 g145
8 g176
15 g (our standard)203
22 g230
30 g260

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kheer, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 150 g
  • Basmati rice, raw: 15 g
  • Sugar: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kheer good for protein?

Kheer gives 3.0 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 40% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is kheer high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 145 kcal to 260 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in kheer?

About 6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 12% of its calories.
