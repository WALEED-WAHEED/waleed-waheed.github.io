Dhokla calories and protein

Also called: khaman, khaman dhokla, ڈھوکلا, ढोकला

1 serving (4 pieces) of dhokla has about 211 calories and 9.5 g protein, with 27 g carbs and 7.2 g fat, cooked with 4 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's dhokla and another's. For 1 serving (4 pieces):

OilCalories
0 g176
2 g193
4 g (our standard)211
6 g229
8 g246

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is dhokla high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving (4 pieces) ranges from about 176 kcal to 246 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 8 g.

How much protein is in dhokla?

About 9.5 g of protein in 1 serving (4 pieces), which is 18% of its calories.
