Fried fish calories and protein

Also called: lahori fish, fish fry, تلی ہوئی مچھلی, फिश फ्राई

1 piece of fried fish has about 254 calories and 20 g protein, with 5.8 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's fried fish and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g166
5 g210
10 g (our standard)254
15 g298
20 g342

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of fried fish, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Carp (as rohu), raw: 100 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 10 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is fried fish good for protein?

Fried fish gives 7.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 87% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is fried fish high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 166 kcal to 342 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in fried fish?

About 20 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 32% of its calories.
