Fish pakora calories and protein

Also called: fried fish pakora, machli pakora, فش پکوڑا, फिश पकोड़ा

1 serving of fish pakora has about 325 calories and 29 g protein, with 12 g carbs and 18 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's fish pakora and another's. For 1 serving:

OilCalories
0 g193
10 g281
15 g (our standard)325

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is fish pakora high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving ranges from about 193 kcal to 458 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in fish pakora?

About 29 g of protein in 1 serving, which is 35% of its calories.
