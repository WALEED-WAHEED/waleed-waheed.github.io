Chicken samosa calories and protein

Also called: keema samosa, meat samosa, چکن سموسہ, चिकन समोसा

1 piece of chicken samosa has about 238 calories and 11 g protein, with 20 g carbs and 13 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's chicken samosa and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g132
8 g203
12 g (our standard)238
16 g274
24 g344

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of chicken samosa, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken breast, raw: 35 g
  • Maida (white flour): 25 g
  • Onion, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken samosa good for protein?

Chicken samosa gives 4.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 63% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chicken samosa high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 132 kcal to 344 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in chicken samosa?

About 11 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 18% of its calories.
