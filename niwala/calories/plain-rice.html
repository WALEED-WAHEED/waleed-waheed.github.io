The recipe behind this estimate

  • Basmati rice, cooked: 150 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is plain boiled rice good for protein?

Plain boiled rice gives 2.1 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 19% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
