Chicken sajji calories and protein

Also called: sajji, balochi sajji, سجی, सज्जी

1 piece of chicken sajji has about 366 calories and 32 g protein, with 0 g carbs and 26 g fat.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chicken sajji and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g (our standard)366
5 g410
10 g454

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Is chicken sajji good for protein?

Chicken sajji gives 8.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 94% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
