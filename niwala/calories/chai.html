Doodh patti chai calories and protein

Also called: tea, milk tea, چائے, चाय, chaye, chaai, doodh patti, doodh pati, karak chai, chai, masala chai, chai patti

1 cup of doodh patti chai has about 100 calories and 3.3 g protein, with 15 g carbs and 3.2 g fat, with 10 g of sugar.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's doodh patti chai and another's. For 1 cup:

SugarCalories
0 g61
5 g80
10 g (our standard)100
15 g119
20 g138

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 cup of doodh patti chai, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 100 g
  • Black tea, brewed: 100 g
  • Sugar: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is doodh patti chai good for protein?

Doodh patti chai gives 3.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 45% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is doodh patti chai high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 cup ranges from about 61 kcal to 138 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in doodh patti chai?

About 3.3 g of protein in 1 cup, which is 13% of its calories.
