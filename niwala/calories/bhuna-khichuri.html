Bhuna khichuri calories and protein

Also called: khichuri, bengali khichdi, ভুনা খিচুড়ি, खिचुड़ी

1 plate of bhuna khichuri has about 392 calories and 11 g protein, with 63 g carbs and 11 g fat, cooked with 10 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or oil is the biggest difference between one home's bhuna khichuri and another's. For 1 plate:

GheeCalories
0 g304
5 g348
10 g (our standard)392
15 g436
20 g480

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is bhuna khichuri high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 plate ranges from about 304 kcal to 480 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in bhuna khichuri?

About 11 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 11% of its calories.
