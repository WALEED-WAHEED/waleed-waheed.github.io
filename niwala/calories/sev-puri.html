Sev puri calories and protein

Also called: sev batata puri, سیو پوری, सेव पूरी

1 serving ( pieces) of sev puri has about 210 calories and 4.8 g protein, with 28 g carbs and 8.9 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's sev puri and another's. For 1 serving ( pieces):

OilCalories
0 g139
5 g184
8 g (our standard)210

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving ( pieces) of sev puri, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 30 g
  • Maida (white flour): 18 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 10 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Oil: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is sev puri good for protein?

Sev puri gives 2.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 23% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Chicken tikka: 215 kcal (+5), 34 g protein
  • Egg bhurji: 225 kcal (+15), 13 g protein

Questions

Is sev puri high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving ( pieces) ranges from about 139 kcal to 281 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in sev puri?

About 4.8 g of protein in 1 serving ( pieces), which is 9% of its calories.
