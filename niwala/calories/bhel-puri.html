Bhel puri calories and protein

Also called: bhel, bhelpuri, بھیل پوری, भेल पूरी

1 serving of bhel puri has about 234 calories and 6.1 g protein, with 37 g carbs and 7.4 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving of bhel puri, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Puffed rice (murmura): 25 g
  • Potato, raw: 20 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 10 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Peanuts, raw: 5 g
  • Oil: 4 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is bhel puri good for protein?

Bhel puri gives 2.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 30% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 serving of bhel puri (234 kcal):

  • Pani puri: 183 kcal (−51), 4.7 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Chicken tikka: 215 kcal (−19), 34 g protein
  • Egg bhurji: 225 kcal (−9), 13 g protein
