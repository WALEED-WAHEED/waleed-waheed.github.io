Papri chaat calories and protein

Also called: chaat, papdi chaat, پاپڑی چاٹ, पापड़ी चाट

1 serving of papri chaat has about 252 calories and 6.8 g protein, with 38 g carbs and 8.5 g fat, with 4 g of sugar.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 serving2526.8 g38 g8.5 g2.9 g

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's papri chaat and another's. For 1 serving:

SugarCalories
0 g237
4 g (our standard)252
8 g268

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving of papri chaat, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Yogurt (dahi): 50 g
  • Potato, raw: 40 g
  • Maida (white flour): 20 g
  • Chickpeas (chana), dry: 10 g
  • Oil: 6 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Sugar: 4 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is papri chaat good for protein?

Papri chaat gives 2.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 32% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is papri chaat high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 serving ranges from about 237 kcal to 268 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 8 g.

How much protein is in papri chaat?

About 6.8 g of protein in 1 serving, which is 11% of its calories.
