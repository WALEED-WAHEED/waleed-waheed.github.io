Mutton biryani calories and protein

Also called: gosht biryani, mutton biriyani, مٹن بریانی, मटन बिरयानी

1 plate of mutton biryani has about 532 calories and 25 g protein, with 64 g carbs and 18 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's mutton biryani and another's. For 1 plate:

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is mutton biryani high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 400 kcal to 665 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in mutton biryani?

About 25 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 19% of its calories.
