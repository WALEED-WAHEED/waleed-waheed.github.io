Rajma calories and protein

Also called: kidney beans, راجما, राजमा, rajmah, rajma chawal

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of rajma has about 238 calories and 11 g protein, with 31 g carbs and 8.6 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's rajma and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g167
4 g202
8 g (our standard)238
12 g273
16 g309

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is rajma high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 167 kcal to 309 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in rajma?

About 11 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 18% of its calories.
