Matar paneer calories and protein

Also called: mattar paneer, مٹر پنیر, मटर पनीर

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of matar paneer has about 308 calories and 16 g protein, with 11 g carbs and 22 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's matar paneer and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is matar paneer high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 220 kcal to 396 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in matar paneer?

About 16 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 21% of its calories.
