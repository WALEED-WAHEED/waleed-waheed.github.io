Badam doodh calories and protein

Also called: badam milk, doodh badam, بادام دودھ, बादाम दूध

1 glass of badam doodh has about 242 calories and 9.9 g protein, with 25 g carbs and 12 g fat, with 12 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's badam doodh and another's. For 1 glass:

SugarCalories
0 g196
6 g219
12 g (our standard)242
24 g289

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is badam doodh high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 glass ranges from about 196 kcal to 289 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in badam doodh?

About 9.9 g of protein in 1 glass, which is 16% of its calories.
