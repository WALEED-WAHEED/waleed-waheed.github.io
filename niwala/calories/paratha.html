Plain paratha calories and protein

Also called: parantha, پراٹھا, पराठा, prantha, parontha, paratha sada, paratha

1 piece of plain paratha has about 237 calories and 4.3 g protein, with 34 g carbs and 11 g fat, cooked with 10 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or oil is the biggest difference between one home's plain paratha and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g149
5 g193
10 g (our standard)237
15 g281
20 g325

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is plain paratha high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 149 kcal to 325 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in plain paratha?

About 4.3 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 7% of its calories.
