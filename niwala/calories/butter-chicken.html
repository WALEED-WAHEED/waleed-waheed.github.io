Butter chicken calories and protein

How much does the butter change it?

A lot. Butter is the biggest difference between one home's butter chicken and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of butter adds about 7.2 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of butter chicken, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken thigh, raw: 100 g
  • Tomato, raw: 50 g
  • Cream heavy: 20 g
  • Cashews: 5 g
  • Butter: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is butter chicken good for protein?

Butter chicken gives 7.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 85% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is butter chicken high in calories?

It depends mostly on the butter. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 226 kcal to 369 kcal as the butter goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in butter chicken?

About 22 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 29% of its calories.
