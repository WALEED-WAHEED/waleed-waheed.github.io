Kalakand calories and protein

Also called: milk cake, قلاقند, कलाकंद

1 piece of kalakand has about 97 calories and 5.1 g protein, with 8.3 g carbs and 5 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of kalakand, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Condensed milk: 15 g
  • Paneer: 15 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kalakand good for protein?

Kalakand gives 5.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 71% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
