Chicken karahi calories and protein

Also called: karahi, kadai chicken, چکن کڑاہی, कड़ाही चिकन, karahi chicken, kadhai chicken, chicken kadai, karhai

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken karahi has about 240 calories and 20 g protein, with 2.9 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chicken karahi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g134
6 g187
12 g (our standard)240
18 g293

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken karahi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken thigh, raw: 100 g
  • Tomato, raw: 50 g
  • Onion, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken karahi good for protein?

Chicken karahi gives 8.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 92% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chicken karahi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 134 kcal to 346 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in chicken karahi?

About 20 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 34% of its calories.
