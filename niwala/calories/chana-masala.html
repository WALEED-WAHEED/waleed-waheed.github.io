Chana masala calories and protein

Also called: cholay, chole, چنے, छोले, chana, chanay, channa, chholay, chanay ka salan

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chana masala has about 256 calories and 9.8 g protein, with 32 g carbs and 11 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's chana masala and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g186
4 g221
8 g (our standard)256
12 g292
16 g327

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chana masala, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chickpeas (chana), dry: 45 g
  • Tomato, raw: 30 g
  • Onion, raw: 25 g
  • Oil: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chana masala good for protein?

Chana masala gives 3.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 54% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chana masala high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 186 kcal to 327 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in chana masala?

About 9.8 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 15% of its calories.
