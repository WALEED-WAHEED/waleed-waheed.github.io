Baingan bharta calories and protein

Also called: bhurtha, baingan ka bharta, بینگن کا بھرتا, बैंगन भर्ता

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of baingan bharta has about 124 calories and 2 g protein, with 12 g carbs and 8.4 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's baingan bharta and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

4 g88

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is baingan bharta high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 53 kcal to 194 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in baingan bharta?

About 2 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 6% of its calories.
