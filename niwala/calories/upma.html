Upma calories and protein

Also called: uppuma, suji upma, اپما, उपमा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of upma has about 220 calories and 5.8 g protein, with 32 g carbs and 7.5 g fat, cooked with 7 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's upma and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g158
4 g193
7 g (our standard)220
10 g246
14 g282

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of upma, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Suji (semolina): 40 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Peas green, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 7 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is upma good for protein?

Upma gives 2.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 31% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is upma high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 158 kcal to 282 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 14 g.

How much protein is in upma?

About 5.8 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 11% of its calories.
