Rooh Afza milk calories and protein

Also called: doodh rooh afza, milk sharbat, pink milk, دودھ روح افزا

1 glass of rooh afza milk has about 197 calories and 6.5 g protein, with 29 g carbs and 6.5 g fat, with 20 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Syrup (sugar) is the biggest difference between one home's rooh afza milk and another's. For 1 glass:

SugarCalories
0 g120
10 g158
20 g (our standard)197
30 g236
40 g274

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 glass of rooh afza milk, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 200 g
  • Sugar: 20 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is rooh afza milk good for protein?

Rooh Afza milk gives 3.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 45% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 glass ranges from about 120 kcal to 274 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 40 g.

How much protein is in rooh afza milk?
