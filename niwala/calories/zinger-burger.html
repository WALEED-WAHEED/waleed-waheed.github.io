Zinger burger calories and protein

Also called: chicken zinger, zinger, crispy chicken burger, زنگر برگر, ज़िंगर बर्गर

1 piece of zinger burger has about 539 calories and 30 g protein, with 42 g carbs and 28 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's zinger burger and another's. For 1 piece:

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of zinger burger, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken breast, raw: 100 g
  • Bun soft: 60 g
  • Maida (white flour): 15 g
  • Mayonnaise: 15 g
  • Cabbage, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is zinger burger good for protein?

Zinger burger gives 5.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 76% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
