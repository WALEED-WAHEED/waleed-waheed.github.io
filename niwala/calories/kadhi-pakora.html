Kadhi pakora calories and protein

Also called: kadhi, karhi, کڑھی پکوڑا, कढ़ी पकौड़ा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kadhi pakora has about 257 calories and 9.6 g protein, with 22 g carbs and 15 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's kadhi pakora and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is kadhi pakora high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 169 kcal to 346 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in kadhi pakora?

About 9.6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 15% of its calories.
