Suji halwa calories and protein

Also called: sooji halwa, halwa, sheera, سوجی کا حلوہ, सूजी हलवा

Half katori of suji halwa has about 219 calories and 2.9 g protein, with 29 g carbs and 11 g fat, cooked with 9 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's suji halwa and another's. For half katori:

GheeCalories
0 g140
5 g184
9 g (our standard)219
13 g254
18 g298

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is suji halwa high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. Half katori ranges from about 140 kcal to 298 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 18 g.

How much protein is in suji halwa?

About 2.9 g of protein in half katori, which is 5% of its calories.
