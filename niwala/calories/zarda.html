Zarda calories and protein

Also called: meethe chawal, زردہ, ज़र्दा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of zarda has about 356 calories and 4.1 g protein, with 62 g carbs and 11 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's zarda and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

GheeCalories
0 g286
4 g321
8 g (our standard)356
12 g391
16 g426

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of zarda, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 40 g
  • Sugar: 25 g
  • Raisins: 5 g
  • Almonds: 5 g
  • Ghee: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is zarda good for protein?

Zarda gives 1.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 3% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

  • Firni: 226 kcal (−131), 6.1 g protein
  • Plain yogurt: 92 kcal (−265), 5.2 g protein

Questions

Is zarda high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 286 kcal to 426 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in zarda?

About 4.1 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 5% of its calories.
