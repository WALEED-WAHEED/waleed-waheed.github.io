Kaleji calories and protein

Also called: kaleji masala, liver fry, chicken liver, کلیجی, कलेजी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kaleji has about 243 calories and 21 g protein, with 3.5 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's kaleji and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kaleji, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken liver, raw: 120 g
  • Onion, raw: 20 g
  • Tomato, raw: 20 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kaleji good for protein?

Kaleji gives 8.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 92% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is kaleji high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 154 kcal to 331 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in kaleji?

About 21 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 34% of its calories.
