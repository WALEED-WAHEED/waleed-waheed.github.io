The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving ( pieces) of chicken momos, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Maida (white flour): 60 g
  • Chicken thigh, raw: 60 g
  • Cabbage, raw: 20 g
  • Onion, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 3 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken momos good for protein?

Chicken momos gives 5.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 76% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
