Lobia calories and protein

Also called: rongi, لوبیا, लोबिया

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of lobia has about 198 calories and 8.6 g protein, with 23 g carbs and 8.5 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's lobia and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g127
4 g163
8 g (our standard)198
12 g233
16 g269

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of lobia, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Lobia (black-eyed beans), dry: 35 g
  • Tomato, raw: 20 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Oil: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is lobia good for protein?

Lobia gives 4.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 62% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is lobia high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 127 kcal to 269 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in lobia?

About 8.6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 17% of its calories.
