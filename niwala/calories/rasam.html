Rasam calories and protein

Also called: saaru, رسم, रसम

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of rasam has about 75 calories and 2.3 g protein, with 10 g carbs and 3.2 g fat, cooked with 3 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or oil (tadka) is the biggest difference between one home's rasam and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

GheeCalories
0 g48
3 g (our standard)75
6 g101

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of rasam, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Tomato, raw: 50 g
  • Toor daal, dry: 8 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Ghee: 3 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is rasam good for protein?

Rasam gives 3.1 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 42% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is rasam high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 48 kcal to 101 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 6 g.

How much protein is in rasam?

About 2.3 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 12% of its calories.
