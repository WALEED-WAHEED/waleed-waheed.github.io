Chicken fried rice calories and protein

Also called: chicken rice, چکن فرائیڈ رائس, चिकन फ्राइड राइस

1 plate of chicken fried rice has about 483 calories and 21 g protein, with 63 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's chicken fried rice and another's. For 1 plate:

OilCalories
0 g377
6 g430
12 g (our standard)483
18 g536

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 plate of chicken fried rice, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 75 g
  • Chicken breast, raw: 50 g
  • Egg whole, raw: 25 g
  • Carrot, raw: 15 g
  • Cabbage, raw: 15 g
  • Soy sauce: 8 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken fried rice good for protein?

Chicken fried rice gives 4.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 60% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chicken fried rice high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 377 kcal to 589 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in chicken fried rice?

About 21 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 17% of its calories.
