Nimbu pani calories and protein

Also called: shikanjvi, shikanjabeen, lemonade, نمبو پانی, नींबू पानी

1 glass of nimbu pani has about 78 calories and 0.1 g protein, with 20 g carbs and 0.1 g fat, with 20 g of sugar.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 glass780.1 g20 g0.1 g0.1 g

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's nimbu pani and another's. For 1 glass:

SugarCalories
0 g1
10 g39
20 g (our standard)78
40 g155

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is nimbu pani high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 glass ranges from about 1 kcal to 155 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 40 g.

How much protein is in nimbu pani?

About 0.1 g of protein in 1 glass, which is 1% of its calories.
