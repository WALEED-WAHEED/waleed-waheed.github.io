Reshmi kebab calories and protein

Also called: reshmi kabab, chicken reshmi kebab, ریشمی کباب, रेशमी कबाब

1 piece of reshmi kebab has about 123 calories and 13 g protein, with 0.2 g carbs and 7.8 g fat, cooked with 3 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or butter is the biggest difference between one home's reshmi kebab and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g97
3 g (our standard)123
6 g150

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is reshmi kebab high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 97 kcal to 150 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 6 g.

How much protein is in reshmi kebab?

About 13 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 41% of its calories.
