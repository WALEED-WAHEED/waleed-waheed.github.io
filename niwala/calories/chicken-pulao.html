Chicken pulao calories and protein

Also called: yakhni pulao, chicken pilau, چکن پلاؤ, चिकन पुलाव

1 plate of chicken pulao has about 493 calories and 22 g protein, with 63 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chicken pulao and another's. For 1 plate:

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is chicken pulao high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 387 kcal to 599 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in chicken pulao?

About 22 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 18% of its calories.
