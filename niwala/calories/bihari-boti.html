Bihari boti calories and protein

Also called: bihari kebab, bihari kabab, بہاری بوٹی, बिहारी बोटी

1 serving (6 pieces) of bihari boti has about 294 calories and 33 g protein, with 1.9 g carbs and 17 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's bihari boti and another's. For 1 serving (6 pieces):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is bihari boti high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving (6 pieces) ranges from about 205 kcal to 382 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in bihari boti?

About 33 g of protein in 1 serving (6 pieces), which is 45% of its calories.
