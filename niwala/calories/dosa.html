Plain dosa calories and protein

Also called: sada dosa, dosai, ڈوسا, डोसा

1 piece of plain dosa has about 168 calories and 4.3 g protein, with 28 g carbs and 4.3 g fat, cooked with 4 g of oil.

1 piece1684.3 g28 g4.3 g2 g

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's plain dosa and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g133
2 g151
4 g (our standard)168
8 g204

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of plain dosa, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 28 g
  • Urad (mash) daal, dry: 9 g
  • Oil: 4 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is plain dosa good for protein?

Plain dosa gives 2.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 29% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of plain dosa (168 kcal):

  • Medu vada: 139 kcal (−29), 5 g protein
  • Idli: 61 kcal (−107), 1.9 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−45), 13 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−45), 13 g protein
  • Shami kebab: 123 kcal (−45), 8.2 g protein

Questions

Is plain dosa high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 133 kcal to 204 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 8 g.

How much protein is in plain dosa?

About 4.3 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 10% of its calories.
