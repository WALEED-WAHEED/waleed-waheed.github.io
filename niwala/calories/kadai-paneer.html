Kadai paneer calories and protein

Also called: karahi paneer, کڑاہی پنیر, कड़ाही पनीर

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kadai paneer has about 343 calories and 19 g protein, with 6.4 g carbs and 27 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's kadai paneer and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g255
5 g299
10 g (our standard)343
15 g387
20 g432

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of kadai paneer, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Paneer: 70 g
  • Capsicum, raw: 40 g
  • Tomato, raw: 40 g
  • Onion, raw: 25 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kadai paneer good for protein?

Kadai paneer gives 5.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 75% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is kadai paneer high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 255 kcal to 432 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in kadai paneer?

About 19 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 22% of its calories.
