Dum aloo calories and protein

Also called: dum alu, دم آلو, दम आलू

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of dum aloo has about 211 calories and 3.2 g protein, with 22 g carbs and 13 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's dum aloo and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g105
6 g158
12 g (our standard)211
18 g264
24 g317

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of dum aloo, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 120 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 20 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is dum aloo good for protein?

Dum aloo gives 1.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 8% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is dum aloo high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 105 kcal to 317 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in dum aloo?

About 3.2 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 6% of its calories.
