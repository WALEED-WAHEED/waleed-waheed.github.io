Aloo matar calories and protein

Also called: potato peas, آلو مٹر, आलू मटर, matar aloo, aloo mattar, mattar

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of aloo matar has about 176 calories and 4.2 g protein, with 22 g carbs and 8.3 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's aloo matar and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g105
4 g141
8 g (our standard)176
12 g211
16 g247

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is aloo matar high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 105 kcal to 247 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in aloo matar?

About 4.2 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 10% of its calories.
