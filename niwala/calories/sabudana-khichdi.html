Sabudana khichdi calories and protein

Also called: sago khichdi, sabudana, ساگودانہ کھچڑی, साबूदाना खिचड़ी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of sabudana khichdi has about 357 calories and 4.6 g protein, with 52 g carbs and 15 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or oil is the biggest difference between one home's sabudana khichdi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of sabudana khichdi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Sabudana (tapioca pearls): 50 g
  • Potato, raw: 30 g
  • Peanuts, raw: 15 g
  • Ghee: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is sabudana khichdi good for protein?

Sabudana khichdi gives 1.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 4% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is sabudana khichdi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 287 kcal to 427 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in sabudana khichdi?

About 4.6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 5% of its calories.
