Jeera rice calories and protein

Also called: zeera rice, cumin rice, زیرہ چاول, जीरा राइस

1 plate of jeera rice has about 344 calories and 5.3 g protein, with 60 g carbs and 8.5 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's jeera rice and another's. For 1 plate:

GheeCalories
0 g274
4 g309
8 g (our standard)344
12 g379
16 g414

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 plate of jeera rice, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 75 g
  • Ghee: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is jeera rice good for protein?

Jeera rice gives 1.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 8% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is jeera rice high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 plate ranges from about 274 kcal to 414 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in jeera rice?

About 5.3 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 6% of its calories.
