Samosa calories and protein

Also called: samosay, aloo samosa, سموسہ, समोसा

1 piece of samosa has about 238 calories and 3.9 g protein, with 28 g carbs and 12 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's samosa and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g132
8 g203
12 g (our standard)238
16 g274
24 g344

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of samosa, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 45 g
  • Maida (white flour): 25 g
  • Peas green, raw: 8 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is samosa good for protein?

Samosa gives 1.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 10% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of samosa (238 kcal):

  • Spring roll: 139 kcal (−99), 5 g protein
  • Aloo tikki: 125 kcal (−113), 1.7 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−115), 13 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Tandoori chicken: 236 kcal (−2), 29 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−115), 13 g protein

Questions

Is samosa high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 132 kcal to 344 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in samosa?

About 3.9 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 7% of its calories.
