Makki ki roti calories and protein

Also called: makki di roti, corn roti, maize roti, مکئی کی روٹی, मक्की की रोटी

1 piece of makki ki roti has about 207 calories and 3.7 g protein, with 35 g carbs and 6.6 g fat, cooked with 5 g of ghee.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece2073.7 g35 g6.6 g3.3 g

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or butter is the biggest difference between one home's makki ki roti and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g163
5 g (our standard)207
10 g250

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of makki ki roti, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Makki (cornmeal): 45 g
  • Ghee: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is makki ki roti good for protein?

Makki ki roti gives 1.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 13% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is makki ki roti high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 163 kcal to 250 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in makki ki roti?

About 3.7 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 7% of its calories.
