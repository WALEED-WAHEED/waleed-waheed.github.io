Chicken 65 calories and protein

Also called: chicken sixty five, چکن 65, चिकन 65

1 serving of chicken 65 has about 376 calories and 30 g protein, with 13 g carbs and 22 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's chicken 65 and another's. For 1 serving:

Questions

Is chicken 65 high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving ranges from about 244 kcal to 509 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in chicken 65?

About 30 g of protein in 1 serving, which is 32% of its calories.
