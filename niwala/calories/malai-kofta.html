Malai kofta calories and protein

Also called: malai kofta curry, ملائی کوفتہ, मलाई कोफ्ता

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of malai kofta has about 400 calories and 14 g protein, with 16 g carbs and 32 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil (frying and gravy) is the biggest difference between one home's malai kofta and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of malai kofta, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Paneer: 40 g
  • Potato, raw: 40 g
  • Tomato, raw: 40 g
  • Cream heavy: 15 g
  • Cashews: 10 g
  • Maida (white flour): 5 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is malai kofta good for protein?

Malai kofta gives 3.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 48% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is malai kofta high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 294 kcal to 506 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in malai kofta?

About 14 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 14% of its calories.
