Firni calories and protein

Also called: phirni, فرنی, फिरनी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of firni has about 226 calories and 6.1 g protein, with 37 g carbs and 6.3 g fat, with 18 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's firni and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

SugarCalories
0 g156
10 g195
18 g (our standard)226
25 g253
36 g295

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is firni high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 156 kcal to 295 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 36 g.

How much protein is in firni?

About 6.1 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 11% of its calories.
