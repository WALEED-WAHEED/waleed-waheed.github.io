Uttapam calories and protein

Also called: uthappam, اتپم, उत्तपम

1 piece of uttapam has about 226 calories and 5.4 g protein, with 36 g carbs and 6.4 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's uttapam and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g172
3 g199
6 g (our standard)226
10 g261
12 g279

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of uttapam, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 35 g
  • Onion, raw: 20 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Urad (mash) daal, dry: 10 g
  • Oil: 6 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is uttapam good for protein?

Uttapam gives 2.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 26% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of uttapam (226 kcal):

  • Medu vada: 139 kcal (−87), 5 g protein
  • Idli: 61 kcal (−164), 1.9 g protein
  • Plain dosa: 168 kcal (−57), 4.3 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Tandoori chicken: 236 kcal (+11), 29 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−102), 13 g protein

Questions

Is uttapam high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 172 kcal to 279 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in uttapam?

About 5.4 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 10% of its calories.
