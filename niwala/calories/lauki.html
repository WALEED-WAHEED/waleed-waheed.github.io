Lauki calories and protein

Also called: ghiya, kaddu ghiya, bottle gourd sabzi, لوکی, लौकी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of lauki has about 78 calories and 1.1 g protein, with 5.9 g carbs and 6.1 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's lauki and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g25
3 g51
6 g (our standard)78
10 g113
12 g131

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of lauki, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Lauki (bottle gourd): 150 g
  • Tomato, raw: 20 g
  • Oil: 6 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is lauki good for protein?

Lauki gives 1.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 6% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is lauki high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 25 kcal to 131 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in lauki?

About 1.1 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 6% of its calories.
