Curd rice calories and protein

Also called: dahi chawal, thayir sadam, دہی چاول, दही चावल

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of curd rice has about 160 calories and 4.2 g protein, with 23 g carbs and 5.4 g fat, cooked with 3 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's curd rice and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g134
3 g (our standard)160
6 g187

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of curd rice, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Yogurt (dahi): 70 g
  • Basmati rice, raw: 25 g
  • Oil: 3 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is curd rice good for protein?

Curd rice gives 2.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 31% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is curd rice high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 134 kcal to 187 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 6 g.

How much protein is in curd rice?

About 4.2 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 10% of its calories.
