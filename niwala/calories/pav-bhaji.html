Pav bhaji calories and protein

Also called: pao bhaji, پاؤ بھاجی, पाव भाजी

1 plate (2 pav) of pav bhaji has about 416 calories and 11 g protein, with 59 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 15 g of butter.

How much does the butter change it?

A lot. Butter is the biggest difference between one home's pav bhaji and another's. For 1 plate (2 pav):

ButterCalories
0 g309
8 g366
15 g (our standard)416
25 g488
30 g524

Each gram of butter adds about 7.2 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is pav bhaji high in calories?

It depends mostly on the butter. 1 plate (2 pav) ranges from about 309 kcal to 524 kcal as the butter goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in pav bhaji?

About 11 g of protein in 1 plate (2 pav), which is 11% of its calories.
