Cabbage sabzi calories and protein

Also called: patta gobi, cabbage matar, بند گوبھی, पत्ता गोभी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of cabbage sabzi has about 109 calories and 2.6 g protein, with 10 g carbs and 7.2 g fat, cooked with 7 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's cabbage sabzi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g47
3 g74
7 g (our standard)109
10 g136
14 g171

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of cabbage sabzi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Cabbage, raw: 140 g
  • Peas green, raw: 15 g
  • Oil: 7 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is cabbage sabzi good for protein?

Cabbage sabzi gives 2.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 25% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is cabbage sabzi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 47 kcal to 171 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 14 g.

How much protein is in cabbage sabzi?

About 2.6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 10% of its calories.
