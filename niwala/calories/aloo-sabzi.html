Aloo sabzi calories and protein

Also called: aloo bhaji, aloo ki bhujia, potato sabzi, آلو کی بھجیا, आलू सब्ज़ी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of aloo sabzi has about 194 calories and 2.8 g protein, with 24 g carbs and 10 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's aloo sabzi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is aloo sabzi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 106 kcal to 283 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in aloo sabzi?

About 2.8 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 6% of its calories.
