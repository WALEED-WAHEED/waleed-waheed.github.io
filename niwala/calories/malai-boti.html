Malai boti calories and protein

Also called: malai tikka, chicken malai boti, ملائی بوٹی, मलाई बोटी, reshmi tikka

1 serving (6 pieces) of malai boti has about 277 calories and 35 g protein, with 1 g carbs and 15 g fat, cooked with 5 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or butter is the biggest difference between one home's malai boti and another's. For 1 serving (6 pieces):

OilCalories
0 g233
5 g (our standard)277
10 g322

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving (6 pieces) of malai boti, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken breast, raw: 150 g
  • Cream heavy: 20 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 10 g
  • Oil: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is malai boti good for protein?

Malai boti gives 12.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 99% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is malai boti high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving (6 pieces) ranges from about 233 kcal to 322 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in malai boti?

About 35 g of protein in 1 serving (6 pieces), which is 50% of its calories.
