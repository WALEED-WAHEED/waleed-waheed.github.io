Pani puri calories and protein

Also called: golgappa, gol gappay, puchka, گول گپے, पानी पूरी

1 serving ( pieces) of pani puri has about 183 calories and 4.7 g protein, with 26 g carbs and 6.7 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's pani puri and another's. For 1 serving ( pieces):

OilCalories
0 g130
4 g165
6 g (our standard)183
9 g210
12 g236

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving ( pieces) of pani puri, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 30 g
  • Suji (semolina): 18 g
  • Chickpeas (chana), dry: 8 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Oil: 6 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is pani puri good for protein?

Pani puri gives 2.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 30% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is pani puri high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving ( pieces) ranges from about 130 kcal to 236 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in pani puri?

About 4.7 g of protein in 1 serving ( pieces), which is 10% of its calories.
