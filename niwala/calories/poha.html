Poha calories and protein

Also called: pohe, chura, پوہا, पोहा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of poha has about 266 calories and 5.5 g protein, with 38 g carbs and 10 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's poha and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g213
3 g239
6 g (our standard)266
10 g301
12 g319

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is poha high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 213 kcal to 319 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in poha?

About 5.5 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 8% of its calories.
