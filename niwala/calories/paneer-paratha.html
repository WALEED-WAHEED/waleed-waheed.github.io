Paneer paratha calories and protein

Also called: پنیر پراٹھا, पनीर पराठा

1 piece of paneer paratha has about 334 calories and 13 g protein, with 38 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece33413 g38 g16 g6.5 g

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's paneer paratha and another's. For 1 piece:

GheeCalories
0 g264
4 g299
8 g (our standard)334
12 g370
16 g405

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of paneer paratha, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Atta (whole wheat flour): 50 g
  • Paneer: 30 g
  • Ghee: 8 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is paneer paratha good for protein?

Paneer paratha gives 3.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 54% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps and higher-protein picks

Instead of 1 piece of paneer paratha (334 kcal):

  • Missi roti: 185 kcal (−150), 5.8 g protein
  • Roti: 100 kcal (−235), 2.9 g protein
  • Tandoori roti: 149 kcal (−185), 4.3 g protein

More protein for about the same calories:

  • Tandoori chicken: 236 kcal (−98), 29 g protein
  • Reshmi kebab: 123 kcal (−211), 13 g protein

Questions

Is paneer paratha high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 piece ranges from about 264 kcal to 405 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in paneer paratha?

About 13 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 15% of its calories.
