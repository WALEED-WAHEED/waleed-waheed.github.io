Mutton korma calories and protein

Also called: mutton qorma, gosht korma, korma, مٹن قورمہ, मटन कोरमा

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's mutton korma and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of mutton korma, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Mutton (goat), raw: 110 g
  • Onion, raw: 40 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 30 g
  • Oil: 18 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is mutton korma good for protein?

Mutton korma gives 7.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 86% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 154 kcal to 472 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 36 g.

How much protein is in mutton korma?

About 24 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 31% of its calories.
