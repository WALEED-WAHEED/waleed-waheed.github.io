Appam calories and protein

Also called: hoppers, palappam, اپم, अप्पम

1 piece of appam has about 147 calories and 2.4 g protein, with 25 g carbs and 3.9 g fat, cooked with 1 g of oil.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece1472.4 g25 g3.9 g1.1 g

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's appam and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g138
1 g (our standard)147
2 g155
3 g164

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of appam, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Basmati rice, raw: 30 g
  • Coconut, raw: 8 g
  • Oil: 1 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is appam good for protein?

Appam gives 1.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 10% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is appam high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 138 kcal to 164 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 3 g.

How much protein is in appam?

About 2.4 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 7% of its calories.
