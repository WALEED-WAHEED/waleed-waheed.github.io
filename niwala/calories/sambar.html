Sambar calories and protein

Also called: sambhar, سامبر, सांभर

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of sambar has about 130 calories and 4.7 g protein, with 20 g carbs and 4.4 g fat, cooked with 4 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's sambar and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g95
2 g113
4 g (our standard)130
8 g166

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of sambar, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Pumpkin, raw: 25 g
  • Toor daal, dry: 18 g
  • Carrot, raw: 15 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Oil: 4 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is sambar good for protein?

Sambar gives 3.6 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 50% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is sambar high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 95 kcal to 166 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 8 g.

How much protein is in sambar?

About 4.7 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 14% of its calories.
