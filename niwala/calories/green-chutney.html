Green chutney calories and protein

Also called: hari chutney, pudina chutney, mint chutney, ہری چٹنی, हरी चटनी

1 tablespoon of green chutney has about 6 calories and 0.4 g protein, with 0.9 g carbs and 0.2 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 tablespoon of green chutney, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Coriander (dhania): 8 g
  • Mint: 4 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 3 g
  • Green chili: 1 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is green chutney good for protein?

Green chutney gives 6.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 83% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
