Peda calories and protein

Also called: pera, doodh peda, پیڑا, पेड़ा

1 piece of peda has about 91 calories and 3.2 g protein, with 11 g carbs and 4.2 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of peda, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk powder (for khoya): 12 g
  • Sugar: 6 g
  • Ghee: 1 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is peda good for protein?

Peda gives 3.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 48% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

How much protein is in peda?

About 3.2 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 14% of its calories.
