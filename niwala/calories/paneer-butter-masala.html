Paneer butter masala calories and protein

Also called: paneer makhani, shahi paneer, پنیر بٹر مسالہ, पनीर बटर मसाला

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of paneer butter masala has about 389 calories and 20 g protein, with 4.5 g carbs and 33 g fat, cooked with 10 g of butter.

How much does the butter change it?

A lot. Butter is the biggest difference between one home's paneer butter masala and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

ButterCalories
0 g317
5 g353

Each gram of butter adds about 7.2 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of paneer butter masala, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Paneer: 70 g
  • Tomato, raw: 50 g
  • Cream heavy: 15 g
  • Cashews: 5 g
  • Butter: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is paneer butter masala good for protein?

Paneer butter masala gives 5.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 71% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is paneer butter masala high in calories?

It depends mostly on the butter. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 317 kcal to 461 kcal as the butter goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in paneer butter masala?

About 20 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 21% of its calories.
