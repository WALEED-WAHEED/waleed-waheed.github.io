Toor daal calories and protein

Also called: arhar dal, tuvar dal, pigeon pea dal, ارہر دال, अरहर दाल

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of toor daal has about 167 calories and 7.7 g protein, with 23 g carbs and 5.6 g fat, cooked with 5 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's toor daal and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

GheeCalories
0 g123
5 g (our standard)167
10 g210

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is toor daal high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 123 kcal to 210 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in toor daal?

About 7.7 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 19% of its calories.
