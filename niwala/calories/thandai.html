Thandai calories and protein

Also called: sardai, ٹھنڈائی, ठंडाई

1 glass of thandai has about 243 calories and 8.2 g protein, with 31 g carbs and 10 g fat, with 20 g of sugar.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 glass2438.2 g31 g10 g1 g

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's thandai and another's. For 1 glass:

SugarCalories
0 g166
10 g205
20 g (our standard)243
30 g282
40 g320

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 glass of thandai, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 200 g
  • Almonds: 8 g
  • Sugar: 20 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is thandai good for protein?

Thandai gives 3.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 46% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is thandai high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 glass ranges from about 166 kcal to 320 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 40 g.

How much protein is in thandai?

About 8.2 g of protein in 1 glass, which is 13% of its calories.
