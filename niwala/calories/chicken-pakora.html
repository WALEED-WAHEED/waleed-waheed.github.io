Chicken pakora calories and protein

Also called: chicken pakoray, چکن پکوڑے, चिकन पकोड़ा

1 serving of chicken pakora has about 335 calories and 28 g protein, with 14 g carbs and 19 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 serving33528 g14 g19 g2.7 g

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's chicken pakora and another's. For 1 serving:

OilCalories
0 g203
10 g291
15 g (our standard)335
20 g380
30 g468

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving of chicken pakora, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken breast, raw: 100 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 25 g
  • Oil: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken pakora good for protein?

Chicken pakora gives 8.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 91% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chicken pakora high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving ranges from about 203 kcal to 468 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in chicken pakora?

About 28 g of protein in 1 serving, which is 34% of its calories.
