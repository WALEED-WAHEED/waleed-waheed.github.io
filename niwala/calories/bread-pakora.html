Bread pakora calories and protein

Also called: bread pakoda, بریڈ پکوڑا, ब्रेड पकोड़ा

1 piece of bread pakora has about 262 calories and 6.5 g protein, with 28 g carbs and 14 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's bread pakora and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g156
8 g226
12 g (our standard)262
16 g297
24 g368

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of bread pakora, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Potato, raw: 30 g
  • White bread: 28 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 15 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is bread pakora good for protein?

Bread pakora gives 2.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 27% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is bread pakora high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 156 kcal to 368 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in bread pakora?

About 6.5 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 10% of its calories.
