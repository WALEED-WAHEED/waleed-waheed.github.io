Achari chicken calories and protein

Also called: achari murgh, اچاری چکن, अचारी चिकन

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's achari chicken and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of achari chicken, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken thigh, raw: 110 g
  • Tomato, raw: 30 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 20 g
  • Oil: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is achari chicken good for protein?

Achari chicken gives 8.0 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 89% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is achari chicken high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 151 kcal to 416 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in achari chicken?

About 23 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 32% of its calories.
