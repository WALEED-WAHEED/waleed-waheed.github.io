Kulfi calories and protein

Also called: malai kulfi, قلفی, कुल्फी

1 piece of kulfi has about 153 calories and 5.9 g protein, with 18 g carbs and 6.7 g fat, with 10 g of sugar.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece1535.9 g18 g6.7 g0.4 g

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's kulfi and another's. For 1 piece:

SugarCalories
0 g114
6 g137
10 g (our standard)153
14 g168
20 g191

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of kulfi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 120 g
  • Milk powder (for khoya): 5 g
  • Almonds: 3 g
  • Sugar: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is kulfi good for protein?

Kulfi gives 3.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 55% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Lighter swaps

  • Peda: 91 kcal (−61), 3.2 g protein

Questions

Is kulfi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 piece ranges from about 114 kcal to 191 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in kulfi?

About 5.9 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 15% of its calories.
