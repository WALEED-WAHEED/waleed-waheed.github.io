Thepla calories and protein

Also called: methi thepla, ٹھیپلا, थेपला

1 piece of thepla has about 150 calories and 3.7 g protein, with 24 g carbs and 4.9 g fat, cooked with 4 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's thepla and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g114
2 g132
4 g (our standard)150
6 g167
8 g185

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of thepla, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Atta (whole wheat flour): 30 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 5 g
  • Besan (gram flour): 3 g
  • Oil: 4 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is thepla good for protein?

Thepla gives 2.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 27% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is thepla high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 114 kcal to 185 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 8 g.

How much protein is in thepla?

About 3.7 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 10% of its calories.
