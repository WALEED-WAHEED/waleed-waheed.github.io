Bhindi calories and protein

Also called: okra, bhindi masala, بھنڈی, भिंडी, bindi, bhindi sabzi

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of bhindi has about 160 calories and 2.8 g protein, with 12 g carbs and 12 g fat, cooked with 12 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's bhindi and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g54
6 g107
12 g (our standard)160
18 g213
24 g266

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of bhindi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Bhindi (okra): 120 g
  • Onion, raw: 30 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Oil: 12 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is bhindi good for protein?

Bhindi gives 1.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 12% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is bhindi high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 54 kcal to 266 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in bhindi?

About 2.8 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 7% of its calories.
