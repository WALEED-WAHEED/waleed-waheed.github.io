Besan chilla calories and protein

Also called: chilla, cheela, besan ka cheela, بیسن چیلا, बेसन चीला

1 piece of besan chilla has about 216 calories and 9.2 g protein, with 25 g carbs and 8.7 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's besan chilla and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g163
3 g189
6 g (our standard)216
10 g251
12 g269

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of besan chilla, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Besan (gram flour): 40 g
  • Onion, raw: 15 g
  • Tomato, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 6 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is besan chilla good for protein?

Besan chilla gives 4.3 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 59% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is besan chilla high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 163 kcal to 269 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in besan chilla?

About 9.2 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 17% of its calories.
