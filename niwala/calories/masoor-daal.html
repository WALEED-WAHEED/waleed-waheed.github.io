Masoor daal calories and protein

Also called: red lentils, dal, مسور دال, मसूर दाल, masoor, masoor dal, lal daal, daal, dhal

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of masoor daal has about 176 calories and 8.6 g protein, with 24 g carbs and 5.8 g fat, cooked with 5 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee (tarka) is the biggest difference between one home's masoor daal and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g132
5 g (our standard)176
10 g220

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of masoor daal, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Masoor (red lentils), dry: 35 g
  • Tomato, raw: 15 g
  • Onion, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is masoor daal good for protein?

Masoor daal gives 4.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 68% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is masoor daal high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 132 kcal to 220 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 10 g.

How much protein is in masoor daal?

About 8.6 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 20% of its calories.
