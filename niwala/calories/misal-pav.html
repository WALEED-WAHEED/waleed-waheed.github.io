Misal pav calories and protein

Also called: misal, مسل پاو, मिसल पाव

1 plate (2 pav) of misal pav has about 516 calories and 21 g protein, with 76 g carbs and 15 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's misal pav and another's. For 1 plate (2 pav):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is misal pav high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate (2 pav) ranges from about 428 kcal to 605 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in misal pav?

About 21 g of protein in 1 plate (2 pav), which is 16% of its calories.
