How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's dahi bhalla and another's. For 1 serving ( pieces):

SugarCalories
0 g199

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving ( pieces) of dahi bhalla, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Yogurt (dahi): 80 g
  • Urad (mash) daal, dry: 25 g
  • Oil: 6 g
  • Tamarind: 5 g
  • Sugar: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is dahi bhalla good for protein?

Dahi bhalla gives 4.2 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 59% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
