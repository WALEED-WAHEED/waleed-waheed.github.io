Sheer khurma calories and protein

Also called: sheer korma, seviyan, sewaiyan, شیر خرمہ, शीर खुरमा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of sheer khurma has about 277 calories and 7.5 g protein, with 38 g carbs and 11 g fat, with 15 g of sugar.

How much does the sugar change it?

A lot. Sugar is the biggest difference between one home's sheer khurma and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

SugarCalories
0 g219
8 g250
15 g (our standard)277
22 g304
30 g334

Each gram of sugar adds about 3.9 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of sheer khurma, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Milk whole: 150 g
  • Pasta, dry: 10 g
  • Dates: 10 g
  • Almonds: 5 g
  • Ghee: 4 g
  • Sugar: 15 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is sheer khurma good for protein?

Sheer khurma gives 2.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 34% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is sheer khurma high in calories?

It depends mostly on the sugar. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 219 kcal to 334 kcal as the sugar goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in sheer khurma?

About 7.5 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 11% of its calories.
