Halwa puri plate calories and protein

Also called: halwa puri, puri halwa, nashta, حلوہ پوری, हलवा पूरी

1 plate (2 puri, halwa, chanay) of halwa puri plate has about 713 calories and 13 g protein, with 87 g carbs and 37 g fat, cooked with 12 g of ghee.

Questions

Is halwa puri plate high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 plate (2 puri, halwa, chanay) ranges from about 608 kcal to 818 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 24 g.

How much protein is in halwa puri plate?

About 13 g of protein in 1 plate (2 puri, halwa, chanay), which is 7% of its calories.
