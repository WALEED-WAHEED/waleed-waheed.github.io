Mash daal calories and protein

Also called: maash ki daal, urad dal, dhuli urad, ماش کی دال, उड़द दाल

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of mash daal has about 195 calories and 9 g protein, with 22 g carbs and 8.6 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee is the biggest difference between one home's mash daal and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

GheeCalories
0 g125
4 g160
8 g (our standard)195
12 g230
16 g265

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is mash daal high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 125 kcal to 265 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in mash daal?

About 9 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 18% of its calories.
