Keema calories and protein

Also called: qeema, mince, قیمہ, कीमा, kheema, keema masala

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of keema has about 313 calories and 17 g protein, with 6.4 g carbs and 24 g fat, cooked with 6 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's keema and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g260
6 g (our standard)313
12 g366

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of keema, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Beef ground, raw: 90 g
  • Tomato, raw: 30 g
  • Onion, raw: 25 g
  • Peas green, raw: 20 g
  • Oil: 6 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is keema good for protein?

Keema gives 5.5 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 74% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is keema high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 260 kcal to 366 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 12 g.

How much protein is in keema?

About 17 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 22% of its calories.
