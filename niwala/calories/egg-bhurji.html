Egg bhurji calories and protein

Also called: anda bhurji, khageena, scrambled eggs desi, انڈا بھرجی, अंडा भुर्जी

1 serving (2 eggs) of egg bhurji has about 225 calories and 13 g protein, with 3.4 g carbs and 18 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's egg bhurji and another's. For 1 serving (2 eggs):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is egg bhurji high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 serving (2 eggs) ranges from about 155 kcal to 296 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in egg bhurji?

About 13 g of protein in 1 serving (2 eggs), which is 23% of its calories.
