The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving (about 100 g) of jalebi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Sugar: 40 g
  • Maida (white flour): 30 g
  • Oil: 15 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 5 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is jalebi good for protein?

Jalebi gives 0.8 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 2% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
