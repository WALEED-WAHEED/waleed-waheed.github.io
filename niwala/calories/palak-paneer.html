Palak paneer calories and protein

Also called: saag paneer, پالک پنیر, पालक पनीर, palak panir

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of palak paneer has about 303 calories and 19 g protein, with 6.9 g carbs and 23 g fat, cooked with 8 g of ghee.

How much does the ghee change it?

A lot. Ghee or oil is the biggest difference between one home's palak paneer and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of ghee adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is palak paneer high in calories?

It depends mostly on the ghee. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 233 kcal to 373 kcal as the ghee goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in palak paneer?

About 19 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 25% of its calories.
