Chicken shawarma roll calories and protein

Also called: shawarma, shwarma, chicken shawarma, شوارما, शवारमा

1 piece of chicken shawarma roll has about 402 calories and 20 g protein, with 35 g carbs and 20 g fat, cooked with 5 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil is the biggest difference between one home's chicken shawarma roll and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g358
5 g (our standard)402
10 g447

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of chicken shawarma roll, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken thigh, raw: 70 g
  • Pita bread: 60 g
  • Cabbage, raw: 20 g
  • Mayonnaise: 15 g
  • Cucumber, raw: 10 g
  • Oil: 5 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken shawarma roll good for protein?

Chicken shawarma roll gives 4.9 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a moderate source of protein. That is more protein per calorie than 68% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
