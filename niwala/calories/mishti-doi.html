Mishti doi calories and protein

1 serving of mishti doi has about 132 calories and 4.9 g protein, with 18 g carbs and 4.9 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 serving of mishti doi, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Yogurt (dahi): 85 g
  • Condensed milk: 25 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is mishti doi good for protein?

Mishti doi gives 3.7 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 52% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
