Lemon rice calories and protein

Also called: chitranna, لیمن رائس, नींबू चावल

1 plate of lemon rice has about 335 calories and 6.3 g protein, with 49 g carbs and 12 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's lemon rice and another's. For 1 plate:

OilCalories
0 g264
4 g300
8 g (our standard)335
12 g370

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is lemon rice high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 plate ranges from about 264 kcal to 406 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in lemon rice?

About 6.3 g of protein in 1 plate, which is 8% of its calories.
