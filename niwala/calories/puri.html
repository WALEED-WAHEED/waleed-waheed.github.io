Puri calories and protein

Also called: poori, پوری, पूरी

1 piece of puri has about 137 calories and 1.9 g protein, with 15 g carbs and 8.4 g fat, cooked with 8 g of oil.

PortionCaloriesProteinCarbsFatFibre
1 piece1371.9 g15 g8.4 g2.6 g

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil absorbed in frying is the biggest difference between one home's puri and another's. For 1 piece:

OilCalories
0 g66
5 g111
8 g (our standard)137
12 g172
16 g208

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is puri high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 piece ranges from about 66 kcal to 208 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 16 g.

How much protein is in puri?

About 1.9 g of protein in 1 piece, which is 6% of its calories.

Where these numbers come from
