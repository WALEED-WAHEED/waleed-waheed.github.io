Chicken korma calories and protein

Also called: murgh korma, qorma, چکن قورمہ, चिकन कोरमा

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken korma has about 316 calories and 22 g protein, with 6.6 g carbs and 22 g fat, cooked with 15 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chicken korma and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is chicken korma high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 183 kcal to 448 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 30 g.

How much protein is in chicken korma?

About 22 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 28% of its calories.
