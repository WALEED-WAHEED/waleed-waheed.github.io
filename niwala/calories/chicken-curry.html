Chicken curry calories and protein

Also called: chicken salan, murgh, چکن سالن, चिकन करी, salan, murgh salan, chicken shorba, murghi

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken curry has about 224 calories and 19 g protein, with 4.7 g carbs and 14 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's chicken curry and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

OilCalories
0 g136
5 g180
10 g (our standard)224
15 g268
20 g312

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of chicken curry, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Chicken thigh, raw: 90 g
  • Onion, raw: 30 g
  • Tomato, raw: 30 g
  • Yogurt (dahi): 15 g
  • Oil: 10 g (you choose the amount in the app)

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is chicken curry good for protein?

Chicken curry gives 8.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it a strong protein choice. That is more protein per calorie than 91% of the 191 desi dishes we track.

Questions

Is chicken curry high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 136 kcal to 312 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in chicken curry?

About 19 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 34% of its calories.
