Sohan halwa calories and protein

Also called: multani sohan halwa, سوہن حلوہ, सोहन हलवा

1 piece of sohan halwa has about 154 calories and 2.2 g protein, with 19 g carbs and 7.9 g fat.

The recipe behind this estimate

For 1 piece of sohan halwa, our home-style recipe uses these raw weights:

  • Sugar: 12 g
  • Suji (semolina): 8 g
  • Ghee: 6 g
  • Milk powder (for khoya): 3 g
  • Almonds: 2 g

Spices, salt and herbs add very few calories and are left out.

Is sohan halwa good for protein?

Sohan halwa gives 1.4 g of protein per 100 kcal, which makes it low in protein for its calories. That is more protein per calorie than 6% of the 191 desi dishes we track.
