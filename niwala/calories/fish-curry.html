Fish curry calories and protein

Also called: machli ka salan, machhli curry, مچھلی کا سالن, मछली करी

1 katori (about 150 g cooked) of fish curry has about 231 calories and 18 g protein, with 3.5 g carbs and 16 g fat, cooked with 10 g of oil.

How much does the oil change it?

A lot. Oil or ghee is the biggest difference between one home's fish curry and another's. For 1 katori (about 150 g cooked):

Each gram of oil adds about 8.8 kcal. One teaspoon is about 5 g. In Niwala you pick the amount when you log it.

Questions

Is fish curry high in calories?

It depends mostly on the oil. 1 katori (about 150 g cooked) ranges from about 142 kcal to 319 kcal as the oil goes from 0 g to 20 g.

How much protein is in fish curry?

About 18 g of protein in 1 katori (about 150 g cooked), which is 32% of its calories.
