Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Niwala for Android, by Waleed Waheed (Pakistan)

Summary

Niwala keeps your diary on your phone. An account is optional, we do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

Data leaves your phone only for these features:

  • Barcode scan: the barcode number goes to Open Food Facts.
  • Photo logging: the meal photo goes to Google's Gemini service, through our server, to name the foods.
  • Account and backup (optional): your email and a copy of your diary are stored on our server so you can restore them.
  • Premium (optional): Google Play handles payment; RevenueCat records whether your subscription is active.
  • Crash reports: technical details of an app crash, with no diary content, so we can fix it.

Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it.

If you uninstall the app or use Delete all my data, this information is removed from your phone. A cloud backup, if you made one, stays until you delete your account.

Your choices

  • Export: Profile → Export my data creates a full JSON file and a CSV of your diary. You choose where to send them.
  • Delete on this phone: Profile → Delete all my data removes everything from your phone at once. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Account and backup → Delete account removes your account and cloud backup immediately. Without the app, see Delete your account.
  • Cancel Premium: in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Camera: used only when you scan a barcode or take a meal photo. You can refuse or withdraw it in Android Settings; search and typing still work.
  • Notifications: used only for meal reminders you switch on. Reminders are created on your phone; no server is involved.
  • Photo logging and accounts: both optional. You can log by search, barcode or typing without either.

Children, health information and security

  • Children: the app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. People under 18 should talk to a doctor before dieting.
  • Health information: your weight and diet are personal health information. We never sell them or share them for advertising.
  • Not medical advice: calorie targets and food values are estimates for general information.
  • Security: all connections use HTTPS. Your backup is stored in a database row that only your account can read.
  • Changes: we will update this page and its date before any change in how data is handled.
  • Your rights: you can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. If you are in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK).
